**Part 4 of my series on why half of new Swiss companies close within five years.**

**What a company does matters far more than where it is.** The gap between the best sector (health, 63%) and the worst (hospitality, 38%) is **25 points**. The gap between the best and worst canton is about 5.

## The hype sectors

Only **46% of new ICT companies** and **47% of new finance companies** reach year five, below the Swiss average of 50.7%. They are not the worst, though: trade, transport and hospitality do worse.

## Why health survives

- **Barriers to entry.** Health is the only sector where qualifications and licences restrict who can start. That keeps weak entrants out.
- **Stable demand.** People need healthcare in every economic cycle.
- **Low buyer power.** Much of the demand is insured.

## Why hospitality struggles

- **A cost squeeze:** rents, wages and energy rise while guests spend less per visit.
- **The irreversible bet first:** a long lease and full fit-out before the concept is proven.
- **Stuck in the middle:** neither cheapest nor clearly special.

## Why the hype sectors survive below average

- **Low entry barriers:** anyone with a laptop can open an IT or financial consulting company, so competition is intense.
- **Funding cycles:** software and fintech depend on investor mood.

## The lesson

Hype does not bring durability. **A barrier to entry or stable demand does.**
