Data tells you what happened. Strategy has to think about what could happen next. Scenario planning, developed at Shell in the 1970s, does not try to predict one future. It picks the two forces that matter most and are most uncertain, crosses them, and prepares for each of the four futures that result.

## The two forces

1. **How fast AI spreads** through Swiss companies. SME use rose from 22% to 34% in a single year.
2. **How turbulent the outside world gets**: US tariffs (39% in August 2025, cut to a 15% ceiling, a flat 10% since February 2026, up to 15% on pharma since April 2026), energy prices, and the franc.

## Why the AI shake-out is my base case

AI adoption keeps accelerating, while the economy grows moderately: SECO raised its 2026 growth forecast to 1.7%. In that world, cheap AI tools keep foundings high in consulting and marketing, many of those one-person firms close within two to three years, and IT service firms that sell hours are pushed to sell outcomes instead.

The wild card is cost turbulence. If tariffs, energy or a stronger franc turn against Switzerland at the same time as AI pressure grows, the **double shock** follows, and the most exposed sectors could fall below 50% survival.

> Scenarios are not forecasts. Their value is in what they tell you to prepare for, and which signposts to watch.
{: .note}

## If this happens, then what

| Trigger | First effect | Who loses, who wins | Signpost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SNB rate rise of 0.25 points (expected December 2026) | Slightly dearer borrowing | Small effect overall | SNB decision |
| Higher US pharma tariffs or drug-price pressure | Big pharma cuts costs | Basel suppliers and labs lose | US rulings, pharma results |
| Stronger franc | More shopping and holidays abroad | Hospitality, retail, exporters lose | EUR/CHF |
| AI agents mature quickly | Less paid office and basic IT work | AI-native firms win | BFS foundings by industry |
| AI valuations correct | Fewer AI rounds | Capital returns to non-AI startups | AI share of venture capital |
| Bankruptcy-law effect fades in 2027 | Bankruptcy numbers fall | Headlines call it a recovery | Creditreform, D&B |

*Likelihoods and effects are my own judgement based on the study.*

> The chains connect. A stronger franc, high energy prices and AI pressure can hit the same small firm in the same year. That compounding is what turns a difficult year into a closure.
