**Part 11 of my series on why half of new Swiss companies close within five years.**

Crossing **AI speed** (slow or fast) with the **outside environment** (calm or turbulent) gives four futures:

| | Slow AI | Fast AI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Turbulent** | Cost squeeze: tariffs, energy and the franc hit local sectors first | Double shock: exposed sectors fall below 50% survival |
| **Calm** | Steady recovery: survival stays near 50% | **AI shake-out (my base case):** consulting and marketing boom, then bust |

## Why the AI shake-out is my base case

AI adoption keeps accelerating (SME use rose from 22% to 34% in a year), while the economy grows moderately (SECO forecasts 1.7% for 2026). The wild card is cost turbulence.

## Signposts to watch

- **SNB decision, December 2026:** a small rate rise would have only a small effect.
- **US pharma tariffs and drug pricing:** Basel would feel it first.
- **The franc:** a stronger franc hurts hospitality, retail and exporters.
- **BFS foundings by industry:** the first sign of a consulting and marketing boom and bust.
- **The 2027 bankruptcy figures:** part of any "recovery" will be statistical.

Scenarios are not forecasts. They show what to prepare for, and what to watch.
