**Part 9 of my series on why half of new Swiss companies close within five years.**

## 1. "Record company foundings"

Register counts include holding companies, among the largest groups of new entries. The BFS, which counts only real operating companies, showed foundings **flat in 2023**.

## 2. "Record bankruptcies"

Since 2025, tax and social insurance authorities must pursue unpaid claims through bankruptcy proceedings. Part of the jump is **a legal effect**, and comparisons with earlier years are no longer valid.

## 3. "60% of firms use AI"

Depending on the definition, Swiss AI adoption ranges from about **8% of small firms to 60% of all firms**, with only 2% of micro-firms using it systematically.

## 4. "AI projects succeed 74% of the time"

Or 5%. It depends on the sample, the definition of success and the time window. [Part 8](/blog/hidden-denominator-ai-success-rates/) explains why.

## 5. "Swiss startups raised CHF 3.3 billion"

Or CHF 2.95 billion. EY and the Swiss Venture Capital Report use different databases and definitions. **Always say which one you use.**

## Four questions before using any number

Who was counted? What counted as success? Over what time? Who published it?
