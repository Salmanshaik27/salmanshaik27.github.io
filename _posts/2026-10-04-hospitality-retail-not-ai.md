**Part 10 of my series on why half of new Swiss companies close within five years.**

Hospitality (**38%** five-year survival) and retail (**44%**) face the highest combined pressure of any sector: easy entry, many rivals, and powerful landlords, platforms or customers.

## Hospitality: a cost squeeze

- Restaurant revenue fell **4.8%** in the first quarter of 2026 (GastroSuisse/KOF).
- Owners name staff shortages, rising rents, changing habits and high staff costs.
- Chains share staff across sites and control costs better than single restaurants.

**How owners could move:** test the concept before signing a long lease (pop-ups, shared kitchens), pick a clear niche instead of serving everyone, and share fixed costs with other owners.

## Retail: squeezed from two sides

- Swiss retail grew only **1.0%** in 2025; non-food fell 2.7%.
- Online sales reached **CHF 15.8 billion**, and 41% of retailers say they feel Temu and Shein (Swiss Post).

**How shops could move:** compete on what platforms cannot copy (advice, fitting, repair, local speed), use marketplaces as a channel rather than only as an enemy, and narrow the range to a niche.

## The common mistake

Making the large, irreversible bet before the concept is proven. **Test small first.**
