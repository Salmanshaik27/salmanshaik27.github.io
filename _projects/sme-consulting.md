## The setup

Unlike most student projects, this one starts without a client. As a team, we have to **find, approach and secure a real Swiss SME ourselves**, then agree on a scope and deliver a consulting engagement. That makes it closer to real consulting than any case study: the first deliverable is winning the client's trust.

## Our approach

1. **Secure the client and agree the scope.** Understand what the owners actually want solved, and what success would look like for them.
2. **Assess the operations.** Interviews with the people who run the business, workshops, and a look at the data they have.
3. **Map workflows and isolate bottlenecks.** Where time, money or information gets stuck.
4. **Recommend and document.** Clear, prioritised actions that a small team can implement, documented so decision-makers can act on them.

> My [startup survival study](/blog/what-i-would-tell-founders/) showed that most Swiss companies are small and fail on how they are run, not on lack of funding. This project is the practical side of that finding.
{: .remember}

## What I am learning so far

How to run a real client relationship from first contact to an agreed scope, and how to keep a team aligned when the client's priorities shift. The client's name stays confidential.
