## The project

A smart system to make waste collection more efficient, built as a capstone project during my Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science and Engineering. I led a team of five from the idea to a working system and a research paper, published in the journal IJSAT.

## What I learned

How to lead a team, split the work, keep a project on schedule, and take an idea from prototype to publication. Together with [Eco Drive](/work/eco-drive/), it is where my interest in sustainability and in the business side of technology started.
